# 1. Definition

> [!Definition]
>
>  Operating systems run in a wide range of computer systems. Sometimes they are invisible to the end user, controlling embedded devices such as toasters, gaming systems, and the many computers inside modern automobiles and airplanes.Operating systems are also an essential component of more general-purpose systems such as smartphones, desktop computers, and servers.
